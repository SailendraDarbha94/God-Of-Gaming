## Who founded Playo and when, what are its funding, investors and valuation, and is there any acquisition, merger, pivot or shutdown news?

### Takeaway
Playo is the brand of Techmash Solutions Pvt Ltd in Bengaluru, incorporated July 2014, with the app launched in 2015 under founder-CEO Gauravjeet Singh. It has raised very little (reported totals run from $0.5M to $2.62M). Its last valuation with any support is about $9–10M from August–September 2021, and I found no acquisition, merger, shutdown or pivot up to November 2026. It is broadening instead: new countries, insurance, and a planned subscription.

## How does Playo make money, and what share does each line bring?

### Takeaway
Playo collects the player's full payment, then pays venues weekly net of a service fee set in each venue's individual agreement. No percentage is published anywhere I could reach. Coaching, corporate vouchers, ads and insurance exist, a subscription is planned, and no source gives the share of revenue from any line.

### Cited Findings
- **Booking commission (primary).** Playo's partner material describes a small commission per successful booking, pay-for-performance, with no rate given — [Partner App blog, 27 Jun 2025](https://blog.playo.co/playo-partner-app/); [Partner Console help](https://playo-partner.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/1070000137391-what-is-the-playo-partner-console-).
- **Money flow (primary).** The full amount goes into TechMash's bank account. Monday–Sunday bookings settle on the Thursday of the following week, net of service fees, gateway charges and other dues. Rates are in each partner's Engagement Proposal, with a monthly invoice for fees and GST on top — [Partner settlement FAQ](https://playo-partner.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/1070000136933-how-will-i-get-my-settlements-at-what-frequency-does-a-partner-get-payments-through-playo-).
- **Commission percentage: only weak third-party claims.** About 10–15%, uncited (16 Sep 2024) [mill] — [iDeaUsher](https://ideausher.com/?p=103554). Marketplaces generally 15–25%, no app named (Jul 2026) [vendor marketing] — [OneOS](https://bookings.oneos.in/turf). Playo's costs described as commissions or subscription varying by negotiation (2026) [vendor marketing] — [Clutchr](https://clutchr.in/vs/playo).
- **Convenience fee: no evidence found either way.** Neither Playo's user guide (7 Oct 2025) nor its terms mention one — [guide](https://blog.playo.co/playo-a-complete-guide-into-how-to-use-it-2020/); [terms](https://playo.co/terms). A targeted search also found nothing. This is absence of evidence only; checkout screens were not inspected.
- **Venue subscription: only Clutchr's unverified mention** (above). Playo's own console page describes commission only.
- **Consumer subscription: planned, not confirmed live.** Bundled benefits, profile verification and more game discovery, expected Q2 FY27 [company-claimed] — [Apr 2026 release](https://businessnewsthisweek.com/business/playo-expands-global-footprint-with-sri-lanka-launch/). A 2019 interview already described plans for ads, in-app equipment sales and a passes-style subscription (>2y) [snippet] — [Bullpen](https://www.bullpen.com.au/playo-ceo-gauravjeet-singh/).
- **Ads.** Playo's team list includes an ad sales manager and a corporate sales and partnerships role — [Playo About](https://playo.co/about). App Store review analysis mentions in-app ads [snippet; page now gone] — [Kimola](https://kimola.com/reports/unlock-playo-app-insights-a-comprehensive-user-feedback-analysis-app-store-in-153745).
- **Coaching and partner services.** Verified coaches for one-on-one or group sessions; Playo-hosted Gametime games — [guide, 7 Oct 2025](https://blog.playo.co/playo-a-complete-guide-into-how-to-use-it-2020/). Academy onboarding, trainer listings, game officials and event organising are offered on the partner side — [Playo About](https://playo.co/about). How these are charged is not stated.
- **Corporate.** Corporate sports day-outs and employee wellness vouchers — [Playo About](https://playo.co/about). Amazon vouchers target both individuals and employers' wellness programmes — [PR Newswire, Oct 2025](https://www.prnewswire.com/in/news-releases/playo-joins-forces-with-amazon-a-game-changing-partnership-for-sports-enthusiasts-302580074.html).
- **Insurance.** ₹9 per two-hour session, up to ₹50,000 cover, built into checkout, about 110,000 buyers a month or nearly 10% of MAU (May 2026) [company-claimed] — [Passionate In Marketing, 29 May 2026](https://www.passionateinmarketing.com/?p=736746).
- **International.** The UAE is the largest revenue contributor among Playo's international markets; no amount given (Apr 2026) [company-claimed] — [release](https://businessnewsthisweek.com/business/playo-expands-global-footprint-with-sri-lanka-launch/).
- **Loyalty cost.** Karma points give instant booking discounts — [guide](https://blog.playo.co/playo-a-complete-guide-into-how-to-use-it-2020/).

### Inferences
- Commission is almost certainly the main line, but that is my reading of the absence of any other disclosed line, not a reported share.
- Insurance is small in rupees: 110,000 × ₹9 is about ₹10 lakh a month in gross premium if each buyer buys once, or about ₹1.2 Cr a year, and Playo keeps only an undisclosed part. Its value may be more as a test of add-on purchasing than as income.
- The Amazon vouchers and corporate lines give prepaid, B2B-style demand that venues cannot easily redirect off-platform.

## How did Playo acquire its first venues and players, and what have the founders said worked?

### Takeaway
The surviving evidence is thin and old: a Bengaluru neighbourhood-by-neighbourhood launch, venues that had no online booking, and 20,000 first-year users through word of mouth. I found no detailed account of how the first venues were signed and no city-by-city rollout, though 2026 data shows Karnataka is still a quarter of the user base.

### Cited Findings
- Playo launched in selected Bengaluru neighbourhoods; many venues had no online booking technology, and Playo took a commission on bookings (>2y) [snippet] — [Bullpen, 2019](https://www.bullpen.com.au/playo-ceo-gauravjeet-singh/); [Business Standard, Nov 2015](https://www.business-standard.com/article/companies/playo-is-making-sports-fun-115112401106_1.html). The attribution between these two is uncertain since I saw only combined search text.
- First-year users exceeded 20,000, entirely by word of mouth (>2y) [company-claimed, snippet] — [YourStory, Jan 2019](https://yourstory.com/2019/01/meet-playo-new-player-going-global).
- The early product hook was social: finding playing partners by skill and timing, hosting games and earning Karma points, alongside venue booking — [eChai founder profile, undated](https://echai.ventures/founders/amit-roushan).
- By August 2021: India, UAE and Qatar live, with UK and Australia pilots (>2y) — [Venture Intelligence](https://news.ventureintelligence.com/private-equity/sportstech-startup-playo-raises-500k-from-ah-ventures-others).
- Home-market density in 2026: about 25% of 5M users are from Karnataka; about 500 badminton arenas with roughly 2,200 courts and 149 pickleball venues are listed in Bengaluru — [ThePrint Hindi, 5 Mar 2026](https://hindi.theprint.in/feature/bengalurus-badminton-boom/941211/).
- Founder view (2026): a co-founder says Bengaluru went from a few badminton venues to thousands, and that people with land and capital now approach Playo for help setting up venues. An academy founder listed on Playo credits it with visibility and fuller courts — [ThePrint Hindi](https://hindi.theprint.in/feature/bengalurus-badminton-boom/941211/).
- Organic growth is still the claimed engine: about 100,000 organic new users a month (May 2026) [company-claimed] — [release](https://businessnewsthisweek.com/news/playo-expands-into-injury-and-equipment-insurance-as-recreational-sports-participation-grows/).
- Pickleball's recent spread: cities with pickleball on Playo rose from 18 (2024) to 52 (2025) to 66 (Aug 2026) [company-claimed] — [release, 9 Sep 2026](https://newspatrolling.com/1-39-million-games-%E2%82%B952-crore-gtv-how-playos-data-proves-indias-pickleball-boom-is-real/).

### Inferences
- The pattern fits a density-first launch: a few neighbourhoods in one city, venues with no booking software, and a social reason for players to open the app beyond booking.
- Supply-side value compounds: once courts fill via the app, new investors come to the platform for advice. A gaming equivalent would be a platform that can tell cafe investors where demand is unserved.

### Gaps
- No founder account of how the first venues were persuaded, what was offered, how many there were, or what the cost per venue was. The three best sources refused fetching. A human reading [YourStory 2019](https://yourstory.com/2019/01/meet-playo-new-player-going-global), [Business Standard 2015](https://www.business-standard.com/article/companies/playo-is-making-sports-fun-115112401106_1.html) and [Bullpen 2019](https://www.bullpen.com.au/playo-ceo-gauravjeet-singh/) should fill much of this.
- No city-by-city rollout sequence or podcast with the founders found; the search budget ended before a podcast search could run.

### Inferences
- **Marketplace alone has not been the end state for anyone.** Playo added community, insurance and subscription plans; Hudle added ratings and venue software; KheloMore added academies. Pure booking with thin capital seems to end in sale or low scale (Sportido, the long tail).
- **Comparing revenues needs care.** Fitso's and Machaxi's revenue may be gross customer fees, while marketplaces book commission. These figures are not like-for-like. Still, the highest revenue and fastest growth I could measure sit with models closer to the customer's membership or the facility.
- **The lesson for capital:** the largest pure marketplace I could measure has reached only about ₹20 Cr of revenue after a decade. A gaming-cafe aggregator should plan for low burn rather than a land-grab funded by venture capital. KheloMore's ₹5 Cr FY25 loss on ₹13.3 Cr revenue shows a small raise can go quickly. - **Direct threat for this project:** Clutchr already lists console, PC and VR stations, so zero-commission venue software is already pitched to gaming venues. A gaming aggregator will face it from day one.

## Is there evidence on booking frequency, repeat rates, take rate, or leakage?

### Takeaway
There is usable company-claimed frequency data but no independent evidence. I found no published retention cohorts, take rates or measured leakage. The story that repeat customers go direct comes almost wholly from software vendors selling the alternative, though the incentive is real: on the unverified 10% commission claim, per-booking cost on such software is about a tenth of the marketplace's.

### Cited Findings

**Frequency and activity**
- All sports on Playo: 600,000+ games a month and about 1.1M MAU, with about 100,000 new users a month (May 2026) [company-claimed] — [release](https://businessnewsthisweek.com/news/playo-expands-into-injury-and-equipment-insurance-as-recreational-sports-participation-grows/).

- Vendor claim, no data given: over 70% of an established turf's volume comes from the same teams week after week, and most turfs shift most repeat volume to direct booking within a month; also that a typical turf sells 7–9 of about 16 hours at about ₹1,200 an hour (4 Jul 2026) [vendor marketing] — [Clutchr blog](https://clutchr.in/blog/turf-business-profitability-guide). ### Inferences
All of the following are my arithmetic on company-claimed numbers, so treat them as rough.
- **Activity ratio:** about 0.55 games per MAU per month overall, and about 22% of 5M registered users active monthly. The definitions of "game" and "user" are not given, and a game may include several players.
- **The pickleball average describes regulars, not typical users:** 1.39M games over 156,000 bookers is about 9 games per booker across about 7 months, if both cover the same window (the unique-player period is unstated). That is far below 5.8–8.1 a month.
- **Funnel:** about 4.7% of those interested in pickleball have ever booked it.
- **Community share:** 45,000 find-a-game matches are about 3% of 1.39M games, so most bookings are not coming from stranger matchmaking, if the units are comparable.
- **Value per game:** about ₹378 for Jan–Aug 2026, down from about ₹416 for full-year 2025, in line with Bengaluru's price decline.
- **Pickleball concentration:** about 189,000 pickleball games a month versus 600,000+ in total suggests up to about a third of Playo's games, which is exposure to one fast-growing sport. Low confidence.
- **Implied Playo take rate:** 600,000 games × about ₹378 × 12 gives very roughly ₹270 Cr a year of GTV. Against FY25's ₹20.5 Cr (or about ₹28 Cr if Tracxn's 37% is applied to FY26), that suggests a blended take of about 8–10%, broadly in line with the 10–15% claim. Mismatched periods and pickleball-only pricing make this an order-of-magnitude check only.
- **Leakage incentive:** at about 10% on a ₹1,200 turf slot, a booking costs the venue about ₹120, versus about ₹10 on TurfAccess credits or a flat fee on Clutchr. Repeat-booking incentives clearly favour going direct, whatever the actual leakage rate is.
- **Design implication:** expect a marketplace to earn on discovery and first bookings, and on services a venue cannot replicate. Prepaid vouchers, corporate credits, insurance and rating systems look like attempts to give repeat players reasons to stay, not proof that they do.

### Gaps
- No cohort retention, repeat-booking rate or measured leakage from any Indian sports-booking company or independent study. No owner survey or Reddit evidence was reached, because Reddit results did not surface and searching ended early.
- International benchmarks such as Playtomic were not researched in this workstream.
- No evidence yet on whether gaming-cafe sessions would behave like sports bookings. Nothing here measures that.

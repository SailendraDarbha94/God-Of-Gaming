## P1. Does Playo show console categories as bookable?

**Verdict: confirmed with correction. Settled: yes, for a player, though Playo does not promote it.**

What I saw:
- Venue tabs read Hyderabad 18, Bengaluru 16, Chennai 4, Pune 3, Delhi NCR 2, Kochi 1.
- The Mumbai URL returns HTTP 404, and its embedded data shows 0 venues. Mumbai's all-sports page exists with 328.
- Category cards carry a Bookable label. Venue pages list Play Station under sports available, beside a Book Now button and a prompt to click a sport for its price chart. Page data puts Play Station in the venue's bookable sports.
- 32 of the 34 visible listings are flagged bookable. Only 12 of 18 (Hyderabad) and 12 of 16 (Bengaluru) are visible without the Show More call.
- No slot list or price appeared, and no checkout was run, so a completed sale is not proven. Rifle Club's 31 ratings since its Feb 2026 listing are only an indirect sign of use.

Why the second researcher saw nothing: their observations are right, their conclusion is not. Store descriptions never mention gaming. The Bengaluru all-sports page shows no gaming text, and its five sport groups in the page data omit console categories.

Corrections, by my reading of names and About text across the 34 visible listings:
- 17 are gaming-led, 7 are snooker or pool halls, and 10 are sports venues with a console add-on.
- Both Delhi NCR listings are pickleball or cricket venues.
- The Kochi listing is a non-bookable turf in Kottayam. The cochin slug shows 2, both in Kottayam, neither bookable.
- One Bengaluru listing flagged bookable (Hakuna Matata) says in its own text that PlayStation is currently unavailable.

Report wording: Playo lists console gaming and shows it as bookable, but does not promote it. On 3 Nov 2026 its Play Station pages showed Hyderabad 18, Bengaluru 16, Chennai 4, Pune 3, Delhi NCR 2 and Kochi 1, and the Mumbai URL returned a 404 with zero venues in its page data. Of the 34 listings visible without loading more, 32 are flagged bookable, but only about half (17, our reading) are gaming-led. The rest are snooker halls and sports venues with a console add-on, including both Delhi NCR listings and the Kochi one, a non-bookable turf in Kottayam. Checkout was not tested, so prices and completed sales are unverified.

## P3. Convenience fee

**Verdict: confirmed. The rate remains unverified.**

What I saw:
- Three Freshdesk articles say the fee is not refunded when a booking is cancelled under policy. Last modified 25 Feb; no year shown, though the weekday fits 2026.
- The cancellation charge itself is set per venue and applied to the gross amount.
- The Pay & Join and GameTime article (modified 7 Sep): fee kept when a player withdraws from GameTime up to 2 hours before; fee refunded when a host cancels or removes the player, or when Playo cancels a GameTime game.
- The cited /cancellation-refund page does not mention the fee; it gives merchant-set policy and refunds in 5 to 7 working days. /terms (24 Sep 2025) does not mention the fee either, and has a blanket no-refund line that sits awkwardly with that page.
- Rate: none on any Playo page opened. GrowthX's 2% has no source; the author says they lack Playo's data; the pricing screenshots could not be read. Two targeted searches found no other figure. One App Store review out of 500 mentions the fee.
- This supersedes the second researcher's finding of no evidence either way. I did not see the checkout line itself.

Report wording: Players pay a convenience fee that Playo does not refund when they cancel under the venue's policy; it is refunded when a host cancels a Pay & Join game or Playo cancels a GameTime game. Playo publishes no rate. The only figure found, 2% of the court price, comes from a learner's case study with no source: low credibility, treat as unverified.

## P4. Money flow and commission

**Verdict: confirmed with correction. No credible rate exists in public.**

What I saw:
- Partner article (modified 30 Sep 2025): TechMash is authorised to collect the whole booking amount. Monday to Sunday bookings are credited on the Thursday of the following week, or the next working day after a bank holiday.
- Service fees follow the Engagement Proposal. Gateway charges and other dues also come off, which the claim omits. GST is added to Playo's charges, with a monthly invoice.
- A player help article (Feb 2025) refers to partial or complete payment through the app, so some bookings may not be fully prepaid.
- Partner blog (27 Jun 2025) and Partner Console help (24 Jul 2025) describe a small per-booking commission with no number.

Rate candidates and credibility:
- iDeaUsher, 10 to 15%, 16 Sep 2024, uncited, app-development vendor: **low**. It is the only Playo-specific number found.
- OneOS, 15 to 25% for marketplaces, July 2026, does not name Playo, sells a 0% alternative: **very low, not evidence about Playo**.
- Clutchr, negotiated with no figure, a competitor: **no number**.
- Inc42 (Jan 2020), Vizologi, PerfectionGeeks, GrowthX: **commission mentioned, no rate**.
- Dividing revenue by a guessed booking value is not evidence: games are not paid bookings, and revenue also includes coaching, ads, corporate and insurance.

Report wording: Playo collects the booking amount, then pays venues weekly: Monday to Sunday bookings are credited on the Thursday of the following week, net of its service fee, payment gateway charges and other dues, with GST added to its charges. The fee is set in each venue's Engagement Proposal and Playo publishes no rate, describing it only as a small commission per successful booking. The only Playo-specific figure found is 10 to 15% on an uncited app-developer blog (iDeaUsher, Sep 2024): low credibility, not a basis for planning until confirmed with venue owners.

## P5. Slot hold

Arithmetic:

- 1.5 / 20.5 is 7.3%, consistent with 7.4%.
- Tofler's 6.57% implies about Rs 1.35 Cr.
- FY24 implied: revenue about Rs 12.5 Cr, profit about Rs 0.1 Cr, expenses about Rs 12.6 Cr, which is internally consistent.
- Tracxn shows a Rs 10 to 50 Cr band at 31 Mar 2026 and 37% one-year growth, but its line items look like placeholders. No FY26 figure is confirmed.

Report wording: Per Inc42 Datalabs (an aggregator that lists filings among its sources; the filings themselves were not seen): revenue Rs 3.2 Cr in FY22, Rs 8.0 Cr in FY23 with a Rs 1.5 Cr net loss, and Rs 20.5 Cr in FY25 with about Rs 1.5 Cr profit after tax. Tofler's free view supports the FY25 picture with a Rs 10 to 25 Cr band and matching growth rates, though its 6.57% net margin implies nearer Rs 1.35 Cr. FY24 is about Rs 12.5 Cr by our arithmetic. No FY26 figure is confirmed.

## P11. Review themes

| App | Low-star coded | Refund or support | Bugs or UX |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hudle | 41 | 23 to 24 (about 57%) | 11 (27%) |
| KheloMore | 63 | 39 (62%), or 44 (70%) with credit and policy complaints | 9 to 11 (14 to 17%) |
| Playo, Sep 2025 to Oct 2026 | 94 | 17 (18%) | 65 (69%) |
| Playo, 2024 sample | 40 | 9 to 10 (about 24%) | 30 (75%) |

Fee complaints on Playo: of all 500 reviews, one explicitly complains about the convenience fee (0.2%) and one about a cancellation charge. The wider fee keyword scan matched 4, or 0.8%. Under 1% holds.

Limits: iOS only, India store only, written reviews only, reviews reach back to 2020, one coder, multi-coded categories. Playo's recent sample is inflated by update waves. A 61% low-star share in the feed against a 4.65 store rating shows the feed is unrepresentative. Rare fee complaints do not show the fee is accepted.

Report wording: In a spot-check of Apple's India feed (iOS only, written reviews only), refund and support complaints lead low-star reviews on Hudle (about 57% of 41) and KheloMore (62% of 63, or 70% with credit and cancellation-policy complaints), while bugs and UX lead on Playo (69% of 94 from Sep 2025 to Oct 2026, and 30 of 40 sampled from 2024). Of 500 Playo reviews, one complains about the convenience fee and one about a cancellation charge, so under 1%. The samples are small and hand-coded by one reader and exclude Android, so treat this as directional.

## P12. Version 5 wave

**Verdict: confirmed with correction.**

What I saw:
- First 5.0.0 reviews appear on 2 Feb 2026. Apple's visible history starts at 5.0.2, dated 25 Feb 2026, so no release note for 5.0.0 was visible, and the visible notes never use the word redesign. That framing comes from reviewers.
- One-star counts: Oct 2025 to Jan 2026, 17 (4.25 a month). Feb to Mar 2026, 38 (19 a month, about 4.5 times). Apr to Oct 2026, 24 (about 3.4 a month).
- Keyword counts on the 43 low-star Feb to Mar reviews: update, UI or design 29; crash, bug or outage 15; game discovery 8; filter, sort or search 7; speed or loading 7; navigation by name 1; support or refund 4. Categories overlap.
- Five low-star reviews on 7 Mar 2026 look like an outage rather than a reaction to the redesign.
- The larger wave on this feed is April 2025: 28 one-star that month, and 34 of the 35 low-star reviews were on version 4.6.2, reporting iOS crashes.

Report wording: Playo's version 5 update (first reviews 2 Feb 2026) set off a clear wave on Apple's India feed: 38 one-star reviews in February and March against 17 in the prior four months, about 4.5 times the monthly rate. About two-thirds of the 43 low-star reviews blame the update or new UI, mostly as general confusion and harder game-finding; filters, sort or search come up in 7, speed or loading in 7, and navigation by name in 1. About a third report crashes, bugs or outages, including a cluster on 7 March that looks like an outage. The wave faded by April and is not the largest on the feed: an iOS crash in version 4.6.2 drew 28 one-star reviews in April 2025. Redesign is reviewers' framing, and Android reviews were not checked.

## P13. Programmatic SEO

What I saw:
- cities-sitemap.xml: 342 URLs, which is 38 city slugs times 9 (all-sports plus 8 sports). The slugs include Gulf locations.
- sitemap-venues-bangalore.xml: 1,233 locality-by-sport URLs (137 times 9), not venue URLs despite the name.

- 24 sampled legacy URLs all returned 200: 21 rendered and 3 redirected to /venues. Two I inspected had canonical tags pointing to a new-format URL and no Book Now.
- None of the 34 visible Play Station venues appears in sitemap.xml. No Play Station URL is in any sitemap I read.
- Venue pages carry LocalBusiness with ReserveAction. Category pages carry BreadcrumbList and ItemList, not LocalBusiness.
- The Bengaluru badminton page has its own SEO record with a FAQ. The Play Station page falls back to a wildcard template with an empty FAQ, and its title keeps the literal underscore.
- Search rankings were not checked.

Report wording: playo.co uses templated /venues/{city}/sports/{sport} pages with ItemList and breadcrumb markup (plus FAQ markup on priority pages such as Bengaluru badminton) and venue pages with LocalBusiness and ReserveAction markup. Its sitemaps hold 342 city-by-sport URLs (38 city slugs times 9, including all-sports), 1,233 Bengaluru locality-by-sport URLs, and about 5,570 venue-style URLs. The venue sitemap looks stale: no lastmod after March 2023, old-format URLs, and none of the 34 visible PlayStation venues. No Play Station URL is in any sitemap, and those pages get only a generic template with no FAQ. Calling this programmatic local SEO is our inference; rankings were not checked.

## P14. Social features

**Verdict: confirmed with correction.**

What I saw:
- The iOS subtitle puts finding players before booking courts, but the Google Play description leads with booking and the iOS description also starts with venues and booking. App name on both: Sports Community App.
- Playo's guide (7 Oct 2025): host public or private games, join from the Play tab, GameTime with filters by skill, distance, sport and time.
- Pay & Join appears in Freshdesk and in iOS 5.2.4 notes.
- GameTime partner page (undated): Playo organises sessions, and claims over 5,000 games a month in 7 cities at over 147 venues. App Store copy also places community-hosted and public games under the GameTime heading.
- Rating blog (25 Jan 2026): five levels, Beginner to Professional, from co-player ratings. A self-rating also feeds the overall rating.
- My reading: discovery looks like filter and request rather than automatic pairing, although store copy says players are matched by sport and skill. Three low-star App Store reviews from 2026 fault the rating system or say it is abused.

Report wording: Social features are central to Playo but are not the whole pitch. The iOS subtitle puts finding players before booking courts, yet the Google Play description leads with booking, so listing order is weak evidence. Players find games by hosting, joining public games, Pay & Join, and GameTime, which Playo's partner page says it organises, while App Store copy also places community-hosted games under GameTime. A five-level rating (Beginner to Professional) comes from co-player ratings after games, plus a self-rating.

## New findings

1. **Playo's PlayStation inventory is recent.** 28 of the 34 visible listing records were created since Jan 2025, 16 of them in 2026. The date is when the venue record was created, not when PlayStation was added, so it shows recent onboarding only indirectly.
2. **Gaming is present but unpromoted.** It is missing from store copy, the Bengaluru sport groups and every sitemap, and it gets a generic SEO template with no FAQ. Both researchers' observations fit this.
3. **National footprint is thin and uneven.** About 60 PlayStation listings across the 15 city pages where I found any, including Lucknow 4, Goa 3, Vijayawada 3 and Chandigarh 2, which the claim omits. Mumbai, Kolkata, Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Coimbatore, Nagpur, Indore, Mysuru and Surat show zero. Results look radius-based: eight Delhi-area slugs all return the same 2.
4. **Other gaming tags exist, with no PC category.** XBox: Bengaluru 2, one bookable. VR: Bengaluru 1, Hyderabad 1. Car Racing: Bengaluru 5 (some are RC car clubs), Hyderabad 1, Mumbai 1. eSports is a tag on venues, including PC-type cafes, and the slugs I tried did not filter, so it has no count. The 168-sport list has no PC category.
5. **Android installs.** Besides the 10L+ label, the Play page data carries an unlabelled 3,462,366, which I read as an install estimate. Indicative only.

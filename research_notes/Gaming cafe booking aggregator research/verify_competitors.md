## C1. District by Zomato

Verdict: confirmed with correction.

Current or leftovers:
- All six named listings are current, not 2025 leftovers. Page data shows start dates of 2 or 3 Nov 2026, status scheduled, state available, action book tickets, and 23 to 28 sellable days running to 26 or 30 Nov.
- The 2025 in the URLs is the onboarding date. Seller records were created on 4 Sep 2025 (Vortex), 17 Sep 2025 (AUG), 18 Sep 2025 (Console Hub), 23 Oct 2025 (Gamers Guild), 4 Nov 2025 (Gaming Station) and 11 Mar 2026 (TIA). Current slot records were created in Sep to Oct 2026, for example 11 Sep 2026 for Console Hub and 26 Oct 2026 for Gamers Guild.
- Leftovers do exist elsewhere. Six other lounge listings show state finished with no shows: TGT, Boomer's and Gravity (Pune), Gamer's Den (Delhi), Escape Gaming and GameCraft (Mumbai). Skycrest (Faridabad) shows coming soon with a notify-me action. I found these through search results, which may favour older URLs.

Slot format on 3 Nov (2 Nov for Gamers Guild, whose page data was a day old):
| Venue | City | From | Format seen |
|---|---|---|---|
| Console Hub | Ahmedabad | ₹100 | 13 one-hour slots |
| AUG | Ahmedabad | ₹90 | 14 one-hour slots |
| Gamers Guild Banjara Hills | Hyderabad | ₹200 | 3 blocks of about 5 hours |
| Vortex Gaming Cafe | Delhi | ₹100 | 1 block of about 13 hours; ticket visible is a ₹600 day pass of 480 minutes of PC time, usable in intervals |
| Gaming Station | Delhi | ₹100 | 1 block of about 13 hours |
| TIA Gaming World | Chennai | ₹300 | Family entertainment centre; tickets visible are prepaid game cards (₹1,200 to ₹5,000) usable across arcade, bowling, VR, PS5 and more |

The page label for all of these reads daily with multiple slots, including where only one all-day block exists, so the label is generic.

TIA is a weak example for Chennai. XGT Cafe, Chennai is better: hourly slots and a ₹1,500 PC ticket of 900 minutes usable across days.

Station choice: no.
- Every ticket item is flagged non-seated with has_seats false. The player picks a ticket type, meaning device and duration, not a station.
- Examples: Gaming Arcade, Delhi sells PC 60 minutes at ₹130, PS5 60 minutes at ₹130, racing wheel at ₹250. Gamescape, Navi Mumbai sells recliner ₹240, racing rig ₹400, couch ₹480. Gaming Kingdom, Kharghar sells a ₹70 Gaming PC hour and group passes by section.
- Several tickets warn that waiting may apply at the venue.

Do slots look live: on sale yes, live machine availability no sign.
- Every real slot I read showed not sold out.
- No free-machine count appears.
- Future days in the page data are placeholder records flagged dummy, with stock 1000 and sold 0. Those numbers are not real inventory, so do not cite them as sales.
- I saw web page data only, not app checkout.

Gaming category: both researchers were partly right.
- Home page navigation shows For you, Dining, Movies, Events, Comedy. The /play/ page lists sports only.
- The /activities/ page has Esports and Game Zones categories.
- The Play long description names gaming arenas under Activities and Attractions.

Wider inventory:
- Confirmed live PC or console lounges, 13 in 7 metro areas: Vortex Gaming Cafe, Gaming Station, Gaming Arcade (Delhi); Vortex Gaming Sanctuary (Noida); Console Hub, AUG (Ahmedabad); Gamers Guild Banjara Hills, Begumpet, Madhapur (Hyderabad); XGT Cafe (Chennai); Neon Gaming (Pune); Gamescape, Gaming Kingdom (Navi Mumbai).
- This contradicts the note in india_competitors.md that no PC or PS5 lounge was evident in Mumbai or Pune. That earlier check looked at game-zones pages. These venues sit under the esports tag.

Traction signal, weak: seller review counts inside District page data are very low next to Google reviews. Vortex 4 vs 212, Console Hub 9 vs 249, AUG 3 vs 163, XGT 3 vs 160. I read the first as buyer reviews, which is an inference.

Downloads: 10M+ confirmed; page data shows 41,737,493.

Report wording: see the structured result.

## C6. Olympus

Verdict: confirmed with correction. Settled as far as public text allows.
- Site says more than 40 cafes and over 10,000 players in its FAQ line. The stats block swaps the labels, which looks like a layout error. It names 19 cafes.
- App Store (2.0.1, 8 Oct, rebuilt in 2.0): one account across arenas, scan a PC's QR or pick a free PC from the phone, live time left, passes as tickets, Pay Later settled at the counter. Passes are bought at the front desk or on a PC there, and the app takes no payments. No advance reservation is described. The subtitle still uses the word book.

- Play description still has older text about reserving a spot and booking slots. Its what's-new text matches 2.0.

- This corrects the india_competitors.md wording that the app lets a player reserve a PC.

## C9. GameBiller

Verdict: confirmed with correction.
- ₹499 a month including GST, or $10 outside India. Free plan up to 5 stations after a 30-day trial. Claims 1,700+ centres, 4,900+ stations, 130,000+ invoices, 19,000+ gamers.
- 1,700 is worldwide or unsplit, not India. The home page pairs it with availability in 100+ countries; the country page lists 101 sign-up countries; no India split was found; the India page repeats the same number.
- Arithmetic: 4,900 / 1,700 = 2.9 stations per centre; 130,000 / 1,700 = 76 invoices per centre. This suggests small or dormant sign-ups. Android 100+ band, 447 in page data.
- Player side settled: it exists. Per the home page FAQ, at /book a customer shares location, sees nearby venues by name, area and distance within 2 to 50 km, and requests a seat with a mobile number. The venue approves or rejects. A venue appears only with stations set up and recent activity. The pricing table marks this as Premium only. The company says it is not a marketplace and takes no commission.
- Both earlier readings are explained: the page shows no venues until location is shared. Not tested with a location.

## C14. God of Gaming name

Verdict: confirmed with correction.
- godofgaming.com: registered 22 Sep 2024, Sav.com, expires 22 Sep 2027, last changed 22 Oct 2026. Nameservers are Sav.com's auction hosts, which suggests an aftermarket listing. robots.txt disallows all paths, so I did not read the home page. One Sav.com URL returned 404 and another a Cloudflare challenge, so sale status and price are unconfirmed.
- godofgaming.in and .co.in: unavailable, but not because anyone owns them. RDAP returns 404, DNS returns NXDOMAIN, and WHOIS says restricted by registry policy.
- Control tests on the .in registry WHOIS: made-up names containing gaming, games or gamer returned the same restriction, on .in and .co.in where tried. Made-up names with god, cafe or esports, and random strings, returned available. consolegaming.in (created Dec 2023) remains registered. This looks like a keyword block on new registrations. Reason not established.
- This corrects india_competitors.md, which inferred the Indian domains would need buying.
- US shop: about page confirms refurbished and modded Game Boys since 2014, USD pricing, free US shipping over a threshold. No street address on that page.
- Other extensions: .app and .dev RDAP 404; .gg and .io WHOIS not found. Purchase not tested.
- One search found no Indian gaming cafe with this exact name; GodGreek Gaming Hub and GodLike Esports are near names. No trademark search done.

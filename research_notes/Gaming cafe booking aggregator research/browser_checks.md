## Google Trends, India, past 12 months (read from the chart, so values are approximate)

- https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?geo=IN&date=today%2012-m&q=gaming%20cafe%20near%20me: the first attempt returned 429 and a later one loaded. Interest ran at about 40 to 55 from November 2025 to February 2026, and about 75 to 100 from August to October 2026, so it has roughly doubled in a year.
- Compared with "box cricket near me": 12-month averages of about 49 for gaming cafe and about 60 for box cricket. Gaming cafe has been the higher of the two since about June 2026, though box cricket is seasonal.
- Semrush estimates box cricket near me at about 74,000 a month in India (Sept 2026). Scaling by the 12-month averages gives very roughly 60,000 a month for gaming cafe near me. Treat that as a rough proxy, not a measured volume.
- City breakdown, relative interest: Navi Mumbai 100, Thane 98, Greater Noida 70, Pimpri-Chinchwad 69, Mumbai 69, Ghaziabad 58, Pune 56. Only these 7 cities cleared the volume threshold. Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai and Delhi were not listed. These are shares of each city's searches, not absolute volumes.

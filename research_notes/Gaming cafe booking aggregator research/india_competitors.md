# Gaming cafe booking in India: who already does this, including failed attempts

Research date: 3 November 2026. Unless a different date is given, every store figure, live-site counter and page description below was read on 3 Nov 2026. Figures on company websites are company-claimed and unaudited. Anything older than two years is marked STALE.

Coverage limit, read this first: the shared web-search budget for this research run ran out part-way through this task. 22 web search queries ran, plus about 35 Play Store and App Store searches. After the limit I ran no further searches of any kind, including store searches. I only opened specific pages already surfaced or directly identified (first-party sites, store detail pages, sitemaps). So "no evidence found" below is often weaker than it would normally be. The Gaps sections say where. No page text addressed to the researcher was noticed.

## Which apps and sites let a player find or book a gaming cafe, LAN centre, PS5 lounge or VR arcade in India?

### Takeaway
The category is not empty. At least six dedicated India-focused consumer efforts exist, plus venue-software vendors with player-facing booking, but nobody has won it: the biggest app has 10K+ Android downloads and the largest booking networks claim only about 34 to 40+ venues, mostly regional. The newest entrant (GameSpotter) launched about three weeks ago with almost the same idea as this project, so the gap is open on winner, not on existence.

### Inferences
- Sum of Android lower bounds for the six dedicated apps is about 21.5K (10K+, 5K+, 5K+, 1K+, 500+, 10+). Playo alone shows 1M+. That points to a very small category, though store bands are coarse and iOS is excluded.
- Three of the leading efforts (Console Gaming, Hash, GoPlaya) are Mumbai-region, so the densest competition for supply is there. Olympus is the Bengaluru-rooted network and GameSpotter is Hyderabad. The geography is my reading of base locations and listed venues, since most players do not publish city lists.
- Company user claims are hard to reconcile with store data: Console claims 56K+ registered against 10K+ Android downloads. Some of that could be in-cafe or web sign-ups, which I could not confirm.
- The structurally hardest competitors may be the ones that control the PC itself (Hash kiosk, Olympus QR unlock). Their booking is tied to the floor, which is what real-time availability needs.
- Hash's move toward tournaments and marketplace, and the ad products at Console Gaming, suggest neither treats booking commission alone as sufficient. No company has said so.
- Ticket sizes are small. Hourly rates seen are ₹60 to ₹130. At BookGames' 15%, a ₹170 booking grosses about ₹25. This is arithmetic on an illustrative figure, not a measured take rate.

### Gaps
- Search budget ran out, so the list is likely incomplete. New apps appeared as recently as October 2026, and more may exist under names I never queried.
- App Store figures were not fetched for GoPlaya, PlayGG or GameSpotter, and iOS availability for them was not established.
- Could not establish GoPlaya's venue pricing or live cafe count: its site renders client-side and its pages returned only titles.
- Could not establish real booking volumes for any player. Nobody publishes them, except Respawna's session counter and the Console marketing figures above.
- Could not confirm Hash's live city list, or Console's fee amount per booking.
- VR-arcade-specific booking apps: a Play search for VR arcade booking returned no Indian booking product in the app IDs I saw, but I checked no further. This is no evidence found, not evidence of absence.
- Method notes: WebFetch returned nothing usable for Play Store pages, so I read them with curl. They exceed 1 MB, so I capped at 2 MB rather than the suggested 200 KB and piped them through a short parser instead of printing to stdout. Those parser scripts sat in the session scratchpad and have been deleted. WHOIS results come from command-line lookups, so they have no URL to open. Store search counts and first-release dates are as parsed from page data.

### Inferences
- Pattern among the stalled apps: low installs, few ratings and thin venue data, followed by update gaps. That fits a chicken-and-egg stall but is not evidence of cause.
- Absence of failure stories may be partly survivorship: tiny apps die without press coverage.

### Gaps
- No stated reason for stopping was found for any product. Why they stalled remains unknown.
- Follow-up queries on defunct attempts could not run. Web archive checks were not used. Reddit and LinkedIn founder post-mortems were not searched.

District by Zomato already sells time-slot tickets at real PC and PS5 lounges in at least Delhi, Ahmedabad, Hyderabad and Chennai, and has 10M+ installs: the biggest surprise of this research.

## What does not pass, or was not verified, and why

- **pgTAP tests were not executed here.** The `pgtap` extension cannot be installed from Homebrew (no formula) and nothing was downloaded from other sources, so `pnpm db:local:test` skips as designed. The database builder exercised all 123 assertions against a pgTAP-compatible subset; CI's `supabase test db` is the authoritative run and has not run yet because nothing has been pushed.
- **The Supabase implementations of `@gog/data` were not run against PostgREST or GoTrue.** Their contract suites skip without `GOG_TEST_SUPABASE_URL` and `GOG_TEST_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY`. The CI `database` job now starts the stack and runs them; that step is written from the CLI's documented `supabase status -o env --override-name` output and could not be executed locally without Docker. If it fails on first push, the two variables are the only switch: removing them turns the step back into a skip, and the failure output will say which query or GoTrue call disagreed with the fixtures.
- **The CI workflow as a whole has never run**; it was validated by the tooling builder against the GitHub workflow schema only.
- **Not covered by this iteration's checks**: Lighthouse targets, the deep-link behaviour on a physical phone, running the player app on a simulator or device, and the two-session race in `create_booking` (reasoned from Read Committed semantics and the advisory lock, not exercised).
- **Founder inputs still placeholder**: WhatsApp number, contact email and social links (`apps/web/src/config/site.ts`), store URLs, iOS app id, Apple Team ID, bundle id, Android package and signing fingerprints (`apps/web/.env.example`, `apps/player/.env.example`), store badge artwork, the product name and domain.

### Confirmed findings, applied

1. **Hold expiry and slot gating trusted the device clock.** `packages/data/src/clock.ts` adds `createServerClock()`: a clock corrected by the `Date` header of every response, wired through `createNativeClient({ clock })` as `global.fetch`, so every Supabase request (PostgREST and GoTrue) feeds it. The player reads one `clock` (`apps/player/src/lib/clock.ts`, `nowIso()`; `useNow()` as refreshed state), the fixture repository is given the same `now`, `Countdown` takes `now` as a prop. The hold screen gates Confirm and Release on `booking.status` only, keeps the countdown and the "expired" badge as display hints, offers Confirm on a hold whose timer ran out ("Confirm now in case the seats are still yours"), and `useConfirm` marks the cached hold `expired` when the server answers `invalid_state`, so a refetch of the still-`held` row cannot bring the countdown back. `history.ts` gains `hasLapsed`, `effectiveStatus` and `isCancellable`, used by the list and the card. Test: `packages/data/src/clock.test.ts` ("a hold the server issued 10 seconds ago is live on a phone that runs 10 minutes fast").

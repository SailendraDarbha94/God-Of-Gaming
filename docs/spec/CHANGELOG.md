- `docs/spec/03-database.md` (Functions, grant paragraph; Tests 001) and `docs/spec/07-standards-and-verification.md` (Database) — after each `create function`, `revoke execute … from public, anon, authenticated` then grant as stated, for every function including `cafe_open_window`, `cafe_is_open_during`, `handle_new_user`, `set_updated_at`, `expire_holds`; pgTAP 001 asserts `has_function_privilege` is false for `anon` and `authenticated` on `expire_holds()` and that `anon` cannot execute `create_booking`. - `docs/spec/03-database.md` (create_booking step 6; Tests 003) and `docs/spec/02-domain-model.md` (quote; Invariant 2) — `total_paise := (round((v_rate::numeric * p_duration_minutes) / 60))::integer * p_seat_count`; `quote` documented as `Math.round`, equal to `round(numeric)` (ties away from zero) for non-negative values; both test suites pin 8025 paise × 30 min × 1 seat → 4013 and 8025 × 90 min × 3 seats → 36114. Why: with integer operands SQL `/` truncates and `round(integer)` is a no-op, so any odd-paise rate at an odd multiple of 30 minutes disagreed with TypeScript by one paise per seat, breaking invariant 2. Evidence: postgresql.org/docs/17/functions-math.html.
- `docs/spec/03-database.md` (create_booking step 5, 5b, 7) and `docs/adr/0005-booking-integrity-in-postgres.md` (Decision, third bullet) — step 5 takes `pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtext('station_tier'), hashtext(p_tier_id::text))` after the GG001/GG002/GG006/GG003 checks; 5b selects free stations in a new statement (fresh snapshot) with `not exists` over unreleased overlapping seats, no `for update skip locked`; `GG004` if fewer than requested; step 7 keeps `exclusion_violation` → `GG005` as the safety net and notes it should not occur under the lock. Why: under Read Committed `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` on `stations` neither re-evaluates `NOT EXISTS` against a concurrent committed hold (so `GG005` became the normal outcome of near-simultaneous requests), nor distinguishes a competitor booking a different hour (wrong `GG004`), and it blocked dashboard edits of `stations`. Evidence: postgresql.org/docs/17/transaction-iso.html; sql-select.html (`SKIP LOCKED`); execIndexing.c exclusion recheck.
- `docs/spec/03-database.md` (create_booking step 5a; expire_holds; Cron) — step 5a expires lapsed holds of the tier in one data-modifying CTE (`hold_expires_at <= now()`); `expire_holds()` is the same statement without the tier filter, returning `count(*)` of `lapsed`; the Cron paragraph now opens with "pg_cron is a sweeper that keeps `my bookings` tidy; correctness never depends on it". Evidence: the spec's own statements (shim lacks pg_cron; the exclusion constraint ignores only `released_at is not null`); pg_cron README.
- `docs/spec/03-database.md` (paragraph before confirm_booking/cancel_booking/expire_holds) — every transition is `select … for update` (`GG007`), then state check (`GG008`), then update; bulk transitions are one `with moved as (update bookings … returning id) update booking_seats …` CTE; `confirm_booking` and `cancel_booking` raise `GG001` when `auth.uid()` is null.
